Questions and party positions simplify each election’s main issues and may differ from actual party lines and votes. In the 1969–1989 supplementary elections only contested seats are counted, without the unelected 1948 members; overseas seats are fixed as they were. Independents are counted as one bloc. The constituency maps were made by reading the labels of the supplied election maps (maps/original), identifying each district and then removing the labels; placement on the map is an estimate.
